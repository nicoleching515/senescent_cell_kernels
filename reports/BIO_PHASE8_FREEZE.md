| Configuration | in ≥1 Tier B module | fraction |

> **Correction, 2026-08-27.** The mouse row read `24 / 35 = 69%`. That pair was a typed-in literal
> (`make_figure_genesets.py`, `gate_disjointness_human.py`) produced by no script, and 35 is
> reproducible under no mapping convention. Derived from files by
> `code/corescence_circularity.py`: 39 CoreScence genes, **31** reachable on the 5,097-gene mouse
> panel through the pinned MGI map, **33** with the documented Title-case fallback for `CDKN2B`
> and `CXCL1`; 24/31 = 77% strict, **26/33 = 79%** under the convention the mouse arm actually ran
> (`run_phase3_n8.py::corescence_mouse`, and `corescence_on_panel = 33` in the committed
> `results/phase3/n8_disjointness_*.csv`). The published mouse arm was **more** circular than
> reported, and the incremental cost of C6 is correspondingly smaller.

**Cite 88%** — the configuration that will be run — and report 79% and 76% alongside so the
movement is visible. The cause is mechanical and was foreseeable: SenMayo and CoreScence are both
senescence signatures, and B7 grew from 35 to 116 genes. B7 alone now accounts for 18 of the 33

`variants/` specifically so the old and new figures come from disk, not from my earlier report.

## 5. Where the arms cannot be made identical

Full audit: `results/phase7_jobA/crossarm_geneset_table.{csv,json}`; declared table in
`PREREG_PHASE8_genesets.md` §6. The load-bearing ones:

- **`REACTOME_SASP` is 40 genes in mouse and 111 in human.** All 38 mappable mouse members sit
  inside the human set; 73 are human-only. This is most of the B7 size gap (108 vs 116) and it is a
  property of Reactome's curation, not of anything we did.
- **`SAUL_SEN_MAYO` is not a translation either**: 117 vs 124, 110 overlap after mapping, 14
  human-only.
- **The two 33-gene Tier A sets are not the same 33.** 31 mouse members map; **26 overlap by the
  pinned map, 27 after the map-gap correction** — `Cdkn2b`/`CDKN2B` is a member of **both** frozen
  sets and is absent from the pinned MGI map, exactly the `CXCL2`/`CXCL5` failure mode declared
  for Tier C. Mouse-only, complete (6 = 33 − 27): `HMGA1 LMNB2 PLK3 RIF1 SESN1 H2afx` (`H2afx` is
  real: human `H2AFX` is in B4 and disjointness removes it from human Tier A).
  Human-only, real (6): `DDB2 GADD45G MDC1 PHLDA3
  TNFRSF10B XPC`. Equal size is a coincidence of the two panels.
- **Ortholog-intersected panel = 2,425 genes** (4,845 of 5,097 mouse genes map; 2,425 land on the
  human panel). Test A8's "report on the intersection **and** on each full panel" now has its
  denominator. Per-set intersected counts are the last two columns of the CSV.
- **`CXCL8` is absent from the MGI map entirely** — no mouse ortholog exists. `IL1B` is a genuine
  panel asymmetry (human yes, mouse no). But **`CXCL2` and `CXCL5` are on both panels** and only
  look arm-exclusive because the pinned MGI map lacks those rows; the audit separates map gaps from
  real asymmetries so they are not reported as biology.

## 7. Environment — the reproducibility claim was broken

`kneed==0.8.6` and `openpyxl==3.1.5` added to `requirements.txt`, each in a commented block in the
file's existing style.

`kneed` is a **hard** import of DeepScence (`DeepScence/io.py`: `from kneed import KneeLocator`),
verified by grep, not assumed. It was never pinned, so a clean rebuild from `requirements.txt`
could not `import DeepScence` — i.e. **the pinned environment could not reconstruct the sender
caller that produces `deepscence_score`**, which is the tool Part II exists to fix and which Job B
depends on. The coordinator hit this after a container reset and installed it by hand. Given that
the file's own header documents a prior container reset and a deliberate reproducibility check,
this gap sat directly under the claim it falsified.

`openpyxl` is mine — needed only to *regenerate* the CellMarker pin, not to re-run any human build.

## 7c. The B6 margin and the panel-definition question

The coordinator's 30-vs-31 for mouse `B_oxidative_stress` is a **real and documented** difference,
not an error in either count, and the gene at issue is **`Junb`**.

- The 5,006-row `XeniumPrimeMouse5Kpan_tissue_pathways_metadata.csv` is the **stock** Prime Mouse
  5K panel. It is not the panel: GSE310392 also carries a 100-gene custom add-on, and
  `genesets/README.md` verified that the union of the two files equals the `.h5` `Gene Expression`
  feature list exactly (the two files are disjoint, overlap 0). Dropping the 9 genotyping probes
  leaves **5,097 genes**, which is what `annotate_pipeline.py` and `build_random_null_sets.py`
  already select. **That is authoritative**, and it is now asserted in code.
- The add-on contributes to **all seven** modules (B1 +7, B2 +3, B3 +3, B4 +1, B5 +10, B6 +1,
  B7 +4) — B6 is simply the only one where a single gene is visible against the floor.
- **B6 is the only module without margin**: +1 on the authoritative mouse panel, **+0** on the
  CSV-only definition, +6 in human. Every other module clears ≥30 by at least 38. This is now a
  numbered pre-registration item (§3.1, D10): any later change that trims B6 by one gene fails the
  gate, so the gate must be re-run after any gene-set or panel change, not only at freeze time. It
  exits non-zero so that cannot pass silently.
